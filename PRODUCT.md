# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are players using a responsive browser-based demo casino. They need to register, manage virtual chips, top up a demo balance, and play slot and baccarat games with clear, reliable outcomes. An administrator account supports testing and oversight of game and transaction flows.

## Product Purpose

Create a mock casino that feels as complete and dependable as a typical online casino while using virtual chips only. Success means players can move confidently from introduction through authentication, top-up, games, and account history, while the system preserves accurate balances and game records.

## Positioning

A polished, logic-first demo casino that delivers familiar online-casino flows without real-money payments.

## Brand Personality

Fun, unstoppable, and grinding. The interface balances the dark, minimal, technical restraint of the Hermes Agent reference with selectively used neon casino energy for play, winning moments, and status feedback.

## Anti-references

Avoid indiscriminate neon, cluttered gambling-portal layouts, and decoration that distracts from play or makes balances, bets, and game results difficult to understand. Neon is purposeful emphasis, not the entire interface.

## Design Principles

- Make every balance, bet, payout, and game state immediately legible.
- Build familiar casino flows with robust, trustworthy interaction patterns.
- Reserve neon intensity for momentum: calls to action, wins, and meaningful state changes.
- Keep the core interface dark, composed, and spacious so gameplay remains the focus.
- Treat virtual chips transparently as demo currency at every relevant point.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA: keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, sufficient text contrast, semantic labels, and reduced-motion alternatives. Do not rely on color alone to communicate win, loss, or disabled states.
