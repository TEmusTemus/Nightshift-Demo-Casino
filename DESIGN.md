# Design System: Casino Demo

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Midnight Control Room"**

This is a composed, dark product interface where players can move quickly from balance to bet to result without losing their bearings. It takes the sparse, technical rhythm of Hermes Agent and adds a controlled pulse of casino energy at moments that matter: primary actions, active bets, wins, and balance changes.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Committed dark surfaces with neon used as a precise signal, never background noise.
- Spacious, technical composition that gives balances and game states room to read.
- Responsive feedback motion that explains a state change without delaying play.

**The Signal, Not Scenery Rule.** Saturated color belongs to actions, active selections, wins, and meaningful status; the interface must remain predominantly dark and calm.

## Typography

**Direction:** Technical single sans for interface copy, paired with a monospace face for balances, bets, payouts, and game data. Specific families are **[to be chosen at implementation]**.

**Character:** Clear, compact, and credible. Labels and controls remain conventional; monospace is reserved for values that players scan and compare.

**The Numbers Are Evidence Rule.** Chip amounts and payouts must use tabular numerals and never compete with decorative display type.

## Elevation

Depth is conveyed primarily through dark tonal layering and restrained borders. Shadows appear only for focused, elevated, or transient states; flat surfaces are the default.

**The State-Lift Rule.** A surface earns elevation only when interaction or hierarchy requires it.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** keep every balance, bet, payout, and game outcome legible at a glance.
- **Do** use responsive feedback for spins, card reveals, validation, and balance updates; honor reduced-motion preferences.
- **Do** pair dark minimal structure with intentional neon moments rather than removing casino energy altogether.
- **Do** make virtual-chip status unmistakable and never imply real-money payments.

### Don't:

- **Don't** use indiscriminate neon or cluttered gambling-portal layouts.
- **Don't** let decoration obscure balances, bets, game results, disabled states, or errors.
- **Don't** use flashy casino effects as a substitute for clear, dependable game logic.
- **Don't** rely on color alone to show wins, losses, or disabled controls.
